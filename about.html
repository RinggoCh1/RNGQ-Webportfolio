About this portfolio

This site is my portfolio for EPC521: Web-Enabled Multimedia E-Language Production at Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM). It brings my e-resume, two promotional videos, digital artworks and a step-by-step magazine cover tutorial together in one place.

Every page is written by hand in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no templates or site builders.
